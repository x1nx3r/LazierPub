## Format

- **Status:** proposed, accepted, deprecated or superseded.
- **Context:** the forces that led to the decision.
- **Decisions:** what was chosen, one numbered section each.
- **Consequences:** what improves and what gets worse.
- **Alternatives rejected:** the options that were considered and why they lost.
- **Open questions:** what remains undecided.
